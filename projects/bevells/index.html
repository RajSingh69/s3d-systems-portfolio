The project

Bevells was one of the smaller business websites I worked on during the first few months of S3D Systems. It wasn't about building an enormous system or adding features for the sake of it. The job was to give the business a proper online presence, sort out the practical website issues and make sure people could actually find and use it.

What I worked on

The work covered the site's layout and content, domain configuration, basic search visibility and revisions requested by the client. It was built using Wix rather than a custom application. That's worth being clear about: I don't think every business needs a completely bespoke tech stack. Sometimes the right result is a straightforward website that the client can manage.

My experience

I worked on Bevells alongside Brickburry, and a lot of that work happened directly with the client, sitting together and making changes. I liked that side of it. You can get through decisions much faster when someone can point at a screen and say exactly what they want. It was also an early lesson in how much time even a small website can take once domains, revisions and little details are included.

What I'd take forward

A smaller build still deserves a proper scope and a clear finish line. I'd now be more deliberate about agreeing which changes are included, rather than assuming something is quick because the site itself is relatively simple.
