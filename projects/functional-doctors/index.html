The project

Functional Doctors started as website work and grew into a much broader relationship. I ended up rebuilding the website from its previous Framer setup and supporting parts of the business's digital presence, including social media, video content and advertising work.

What I built

The new site uses React and Vite. It has detailed practitioner pages, information about services and testing, pricing, FAQs, partner content and a way for visitors to enquire or book. A big part of the job was presenting quite a lot of medical and service information without making the website feel cluttered.

Beyond the website

Alongside the site I've supported the practice's social media, video content and advertising. A website doesn't exist on its own when the business is also running campaigns, publishing content and handling enquiries.

My experience

By this stage I felt much more comfortable putting together a complete website. I enjoyed the design direction and making all the content work across desktop, tablet and mobile. What made this different was the ongoing support: a website doesn't exist on its own when the business is also running campaigns, publishing content and handling enquiries.

What I'd take forward

The main lesson was keeping development work and monthly marketing support clearly defined. When you're involved in several parts of a client's business, it's easy for every request to become “just one more thing”. I'd now make responsibilities and ongoing work clearer from the start.
