The project

Created for Guru Nanak Gurdwara Camberley as part of a drone and robotics class for children aged 5–13. The course introduced safe drone flying using a DJI Neo, then moved into coding, robotics thinking, planning, structure and design.

Under my guidance, the children planned, built and programmed a working robot designed to move food around the langar hall. The next stage is to rebuild the concept with stronger components and potentially a microcontroller-based system.
