The project

Pyroworx was about replacing repetitive paperwork with a system that matched how the business actually operated. It involved invoices, customer and product records, delivery notes and the information needed to generate those documents consistently.

How it works

Rather than rebuilding each invoice or delivery note manually, the system stores reusable information and uses it to prepare the documents. There are customer and product records, a dashboard, login and spreadsheet import/export. The aim is simple: enter information in the right place once, then use it properly.

Framework

Where the work really is

The important engineering work here is the data structure, the document templates and getting the workflow right for the person using it.

My experience

This project taught me a lesson I keep coming back to: just because software can do something doesn't mean it's making someone's day easier. I liked turning the business's existing paperwork into a working system, but I also realised that the real specification is how the staff already do the job, not how I imagine they should do it.

What I'd take forward

I'd spend more time observing the existing process before designing the screens. The best outcome isn't the most elaborate dashboard; it's a system that removes unnecessary steps and produces the right documents without fuss.
