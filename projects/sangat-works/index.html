The project

Sangat Works is one of my own products, built with Jags. The idea was to create a useful space for Sikh professionals and businesses to connect, find opportunities and support one another, rather than relying on scattered social media posts or word of mouth.

How it works

Members can create profiles and appear in a business/professional directory. The platform also has a map, messaging, opportunities, a marketplace, learning and community sections, and areas for Gurdwaras and young professionals. Membership and featured-listing payments are handled through Stripe, with an admin side for managing the platform.

Platform

The public frontend uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Functions behind it. Stripe handles subscriptions.

After launch

This was a different challenge from building software for one client because I also had to think about what happens after launch: getting people to join, keeping information useful and giving them a reason to return.

My experience

I liked that Sangat Works was ours to build and shape. There wasn't a client handing over a fixed list of requirements. We had an idea, worked out what it needed and turned it into something people could actually use. It was also my first real experience of a platform earning money through its own memberships, which felt different from sending an invoice for a finished website.

What I'd take forward

Building features is only half of building a platform. The other half is getting the community involved. I'd focus just as much on the value members get every week as on adding another feature to the dashboard.
