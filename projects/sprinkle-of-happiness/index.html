The project

Sprinkle of Happiness was one of the most design-heavy websites I worked on during the year. It needed to present a creative project with its own personality, programmes and activities, rather than looking like a standard business template.

What I built

The site includes multiple content pages, programme information, workshops, calendar material, accessibility information and custom visual effects. There was a lot of attention to layout, animation and how the content felt as you moved through the site. It was custom-built and published using GitHub Pages with a custom domain.

My experience

I genuinely liked the creative side of this project. It gave me a chance to do something more expressive than a business dashboard and spend time on presentation, movement and detail. I think some of the design work was among my strongest that year. It also involved a lot of revisions, which taught me how important it is to keep the creative brief and the practical scope aligned.

What I'd take forward

Creative projects need just as much structure as technical ones. I'd agree the content, page list and revision process earlier so that design work can stay focused instead of constantly being reopened.
