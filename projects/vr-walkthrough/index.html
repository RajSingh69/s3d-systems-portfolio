The project

This Android VR app lets users walk through the planned GNGC library before the room is physically built. The space was designed from a single blueprint, modelled to scale, then converted into a real-time walkthrough.

It works with a Google Cardboard-style screen and a Bluetooth controller such as a PS5 controller, Xbox controller or generic gamepad, allowing users to look around and move through the digital room.

The workflow

Step by step

  1. A single blueprint of the planned library
  2. The room modelled to scale
  3. Converted into a real-time walkthrough
  4. Explored in a headset with a controller
