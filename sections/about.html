About S3D Systems

Independent, technical and building without limits.

S3D Systems is a bespoke software development business I founded in November 2025. I’m Rajan Bhamra, a Computer Science graduate from Surrey, and you work directly with me: the person who designs, builds, deploys and supports your software.

Flexible, practical and idea-driven.

I studied Computer Science at Swansea University, graduated in July 2024 and worked as a developer in professional employment before leaving in October 2025 to build software independently. Every S3D Systems project since has been designed, built, deployed and supported by me, end to end.


That work spans PHP and MySQL business systems, a Laravel invoicing platform, Firebase-backed portals with logins and secure links, React websites, Flutter apps published to the App Store and Google Play, and membership platforms with Stripe payments, plus the hosting, domains, deployment and support around them.


S3D Systems builds custom business software and internal systems, websites and web apps, mobile apps, automation and API integrations, secure portals and databases, and keeps them running with ongoing support. Alongside that: 3D modelling, drone filming and social media content.


I’m flexible on pricing because not every client or project is the same. Scope and price are agreed before development starts, and larger systems can be built in phases.
